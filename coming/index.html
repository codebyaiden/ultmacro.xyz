What’s Coming

Major changes and everyday improvements we're planning for Ultimate Macro. No fake dates or promised release windows.

MAJOR

AI Assistant Integration

Connect Ultimate Macro to ChatGPT or another AI provider to understand your run analytics, troubleshoot errors, and plan your next sessions. Remote actions would always require your approval.

Ultimate Cloud & Accounts

Keep your settings, strategies, and progress together. Sign in to manage your setup and sync supported data across devices.

Smart Recording System

Record strategies more reliably, spot mistakes, and make captured runs easier to review and improve.

AI-Powered Goals

Choose an achievable goal, like unlocking a tower or earning a set amount. The AI would plan, launch and adapt supported runs to reach it automatically, within the limits you approve.

Phone & Watch Control

Check a run, pause, resume, or stop it from your phone or smartwatch, with secure pairing and clear controls.

macOS Support

Bring Ultimate Macro to Mac. This requires a separate implementation beyond the current Windows/AutoHotkey build.

More Game Support

Explore dedicated macro support for games beyond TDS, including Bee Swarm Simulator and Murder Mystery 2.

USEFUL

Automatic Updates

Download and install updates directly inside Ultimate Macro instead of revisiting the website every time. Faster, easier, and less manual work.

Automations

Create simple routines and conditions for your runs, with safe controls for what starts, stops, and repeats.

Multi-Language Support

Use the app and its setup guidance in more languages, without relying on translated instructions from other users.

These features are plans, not part of the current public release. Details and priorities may change. AI integrations would be optional and are not affiliated with or endorsed by OpenAI.
