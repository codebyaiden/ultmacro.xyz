What's coming.

Accounts, smarter recording, remote control, more platforms, and tools built around your runs.

These are planned directions, not released features. Details and priorities can change. No release dates are confirmed.

The big changes

A different kind of macro.

01 / Your spacePlanned

Ultimate Cloud & accounts.

A personal place for your setup, saved strategies and progress. An account system would connect it all when you choose to sign in.

Ultimate CloudAccount system
02 / Smarter runsPlanned

Recording that thinks ahead.

Smarter recording to help refine strategies, plus AI-powered goals that turn what you want to achieve into a clearer plan.

Smart recordingAI-powered goals
03 / Remote controlPlanned

Control beyond your PC.

Check and control a run from your phone or watch. We're also exploring MCP connections so AI assistants like ChatGPT could help troubleshoot and plan, with your approval before actions.

PhoneWatchAI assistant / MCP
04 / Your workflowPlanned

Automations you control.

Set up repeatable routines and conditions around your sessions without rebuilding the same workflow every time. Clear pause and stop controls remain essential.

AutomationsCustom workflows

Further out

Beyond Windows. Beyond TDS.

Larger expansions we want to explore, with more technical work and no announced timeline.

macOS support

Bring Ultimate Macro to Mac users. This needs more than repackaging the current Windows and AutoHotkey implementation.

More games

Game-specific macro support beyond Tower Defense Simulator, including ideas for Bee Swarm Simulator and Murder Mystery 2. Neither is supported by the current public release.
