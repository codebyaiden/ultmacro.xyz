v1.3.6 release notes

New

  • Automatic playtime reward collection.
  • Optional setting to open Ultimate Macro when your computer starts.
  • Click or scroll the survival icon during Easy Voidcore runs.

Run reliability

  • Upgrade timing waits for enough cash and continues if the displayed cash value stops updating (beta).
  • Detect invalid tower placements sooner and save confirmed fixes into the active strategy for later runs.

Strategy support

  • Choose any map with the override selector.
  • Create strategies for Solar Requiem Night 1 on Easy or Hard using the recorded loadout (beta).

Improvements

  • Auto Equip accuracy and speed improvements.
  • Quality-of-life changes and fixes from earlier releases.

Auto Equip: It may still miss towers. Check your loadout before starting a match.

Shard pickup and Logbook collection are still in development.
