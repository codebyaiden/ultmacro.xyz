What changed

  • Added playtime rewards collection
  • All-map support for the override map selector
  • The macro now waits for you to get enough money to upgrade your tower and does NOT stop the entire macro if it doesn't update anymore (beta)
  • If there's a wrong tower spot, but later there's a fixed one, the macro will automatically update your strategy file you're using with the macro for all of the next runs
  • The macro now detects wrong spots faster instead of placing the tower like 3-4 times until it says 'can't place tower here', was tested and it detects it at the first 'cant place' or second
  • The macro now can click on the survival icon // scroll whenever you're macroing Easy - Voidcore
  • Auto Equip improvements on accuracy and speed
  • "Open Macro when your computer is powered on" setting
  • You can now create strategies with the Solar Requiem Event modes, for now it is ONLY night 1 (on both difficulties) and it chooses the correct loadout you put via recording tab (beta)
  • Small QOL improvements
  • Huge bug fixes from other versions

Shard pickup and Logbook collection are still in development. Thanks for your patience!

Auto Equip notice: Auto Equip may still miss towers. Check your loadout before starting a match.
