Setup guide/Recording a strategy

Recording a strategy

Record a run and save it as a strategy you can replay.

You never have to write a strategy file by hand. Open Create and choose Record a Strategy, play a run normally, and save the result as a replayable .strat file. Choose Edit a Strategy or Open Strategy Lab when you need to inspect or refine an existing file.

Before you press record

  • Map and Mode — pick them from the dropdowns. Modes include Easy, Casual, Intermediate, Molten, Fallen, Frost, Hardcore, Voidcore and Arcade, plus the map-specific ones.
  • Towers — a comma-separated list, e.g. Minigunner, Ranger, Commander, DJ. Prefix a tower with G (as in G Minigunner) if the strategy needs the golden version.
  • Modifiers — hold Ctrl to select or deselect more than one.
  • Ability toggles — Use Call of Arms, Use Support Caravan and Use Drop the Beat, plus Auto Skip Waves and Abilities Spam.
  • Move — optionally walk your character in a direction for a set number of milliseconds at the start of a run.

Recording hotkeys

These are the defaults; all of them are rebindable under Settings → Recording Hotkeys.

ActionDefaultNotes
Place TowerF Records the placement at the cursor.
Upgrade TowerCtrl + U Split upgrade paths are supported — top and bottom are recorded separately.
Sell TowerCtrl + X
Change TargetsCtrl + ` Records a tower targeting change.
Change DJ TrackCtrl + D Records a DJ disc change. Discs can also be put on a wave-based schedule so they switch automatically.
Align CameraCtrl + T
Hologram TowerCtrl + Alt + H
Delete RecordCtrl + B Removes the last recorded action.
Record InputsCtrl + Shift + E

Raise the Dead (Necromancer) and Brawler reposition are recorded by pressing Ctrl together with your in-game keybind for them, rather than a separate recording hotkey.

Recording well

  • Play deliberately. The recorder captures what you actually did, so a messy run replays as a messy run.
  • Consider a timescale ticket when recording a long or complex strategy — the macro's own tutorial recommends it.
  • Name it for what it does, e.g. pw2_solo_farm.strat. Future you will not remember strat3_final_v2.strat.
