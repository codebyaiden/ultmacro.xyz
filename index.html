Good to know.

Is it free?

Yes. Public releases are free and open source.

What do I need?

Windows 10 or 11, AutoHotkey v2 and the required display, Roblox and OCR settings. See the setup guide.

Can it run unattended?

Some runs support restarts and reconnection, but screen detection can fail. Test your loadout and watch a full match before leaving it running.

Where do I find strategies?

Browse the built-in strategy library or import a compatible .strat file in the app.
