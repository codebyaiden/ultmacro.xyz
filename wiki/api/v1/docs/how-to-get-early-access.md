> Join through Ko-fi, understand supporter benefits, and check limited-time giveaways or contributor invitations.

## Get Early Access through Ko-fi

Early Access is an optional supporter membership. The public Ultimate Macro remains free and open source.

1. Open the official Ultimate Macro Early Access page at https://ultmacro.xyz/early-access/.
2. Select Get Early Access to continue to the official Ko-fi membership checkout.
3. Join for $1.99 USD per month. Ko-fi handles membership payments and cancellation.
4. Join the official Ultimate Macro Discord at https://discord.gg/ultmacro and follow the supporter access instructions. If you do not see the Early Access channels or role, contact the team through Discord.

## What supporters receive

Supporter access includes early builds before the public release, private supporter announcements and chat, and an exclusive green Early Access Discord role. Builds are previews and may be less stable than public releases.

- Use only the download instructions shared through official supporter channels.
- Keep unreleased Early Access files inside the server until they are public.

## Giveaways and other opportunities

The team may offer limited-time Early Access through official Discord giveaways or special contributions. These opportunities are not always open and are not guaranteed by joining the server or submitting a contribution.

1. Watch official announcements and giveaways in the Ultimate Macro Discord.
2. Follow the conditions and claim instructions for the specific event.
3. If you win or are granted access, ask staff when your access begins and how long it lasts. Some giveaway awards have offered 14 days, but the event announcement determines the actual duration and activation options.

## Membership, cancellation and missing access

Ko-fi memberships are monthly and can be cancelled through Ko-fi. Cancelling does not change the availability of the free public Ultimate Macro releases.

- If your supporter role or channels are missing, confirm the membership is active and follow the official Discord supporter instructions.
- Contact staff through the official Discord for help with activation. Do not post receipts, payment information or account credentials publicly.
- Do not purchase Early Access from third-party sellers or download leaked prerelease builds.
