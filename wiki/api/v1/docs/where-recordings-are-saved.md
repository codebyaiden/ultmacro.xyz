> Find personal .strat recordings and the bundled strategy directory.

## Two different directories

The released Main.ahk defines a user-specific Recordings folder and a separate Resources\Strats folder next to the macro.

| Setting | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Recorded strategies | %APPDATA%\Ultimate_Macro\Recordings |
| Bundled strategy files | <macro folder>\Resources\Strats |

## Find your files

1. Press Win + R and open %APPDATA%\Ultimate_Macro\Recordings.
2. Sort by modification date to find your latest recording.
3. For bundled strategies, open the extracted macro folder, then Resources and Strats.
4. Make a copy of any strategy before editing it in Strategy Lab.
